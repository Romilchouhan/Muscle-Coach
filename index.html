How to use this plan

Keep most working sets around 1–3 reps in reserve. When you reach the top of the rep range with clean form for every set, add a small amount of weight next time. Rest around 2–3 minutes on compound lifts and 60–90 seconds on isolation work. Warm-up sets do not count as working sets.
